Setter (First Point of Contact)

The Setter acts as the first point of contact for all new and re-engaged leads. Their primary goal is to build rapport, answer questions, and move prospects forward through scheduling qualified calls for Closers.

Core Responsibilities

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • Median lead response time

Closer (Sales Conversion)

The Closer is responsible for converting scheduled calls into enrolled students by leading high-quality sales conversations that include detailed discovery, clear offer presentation, objection resolution, and confident decision-making.

Core Responsibilities

  • Handle objections and guide prospects to confident decisions

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • Overall conversion rate
  • Inbound call answer rate
  • Outbound call activity
  • Conversation quality & objection handling

Sales Framework & Call Structure

The Sales Playbook: Setter → Closer Pipeline

Our sales framework is built on a two-tier system designed to move leads efficiently from initial contact through enrollment:
  1. SETTER — First point of contact. Builds rapport, qualifies leads, schedules calls.
  2. CLOSER — Converts calls to enrollments. Leads full sales conversations with discovery → offer → close.

Sales Process Flow

Intro → Discovery → Offer → Close

Every sales conversation follows this sequence. The Setter handles intro/initial discovery. The Closer handles full discovery, offer presentation, objection resolution, and closing.

8-Step Call Framework

  1. State their name: "Hi [Name]…"
  2. State who you are & where you're calling from: "This is [Your Name] with Accelerated Academy…"
  3. State why you're calling: Reference their action (application step, Facebook interest, etc.)
  4. Ask a question to engage: Don't stop after introducing yourself
  5. Check if it's a good time: Respect their time
  6. Tone matters: Smile, be warm, confident, energetic (not monotone/robotic)
  7. Assume they know nothing: Explain clearly and simply
  8. Lead the conversation: Guide them through next steps

Strong Examples

"Hi Sarah, this is Carley with Accelerated Academy, the 10-week dental assisting program in Baton Rouge. How are you? Great! I am calling because I saw you started your application online for our Fall course and made it to the second step, so I wanted to reach out and help with anything you may need. Do you have a quick minute to talk?"
"Hi Alex, this is Carley with Accelerated Academy, the 10-week dental assisting course in Cary - how are you today? Awesome, I am reaching out because I saw you showed interest on Facebook, so I wanted to follow up and see if you had any questions about the program - or if you'd like me to go over some course details with you. Is now a good time?"

Building Rapport & Discovery

Core Goal: Clearly understand each prospect's motivation, situation, and readiness for change.

Key Discovery Questions

  • Do they want to be a Dental Assistant? What appeals to them about the role?
  • Why do they want to be a DA? What's driving this specific interest?
  • What are they currently doing for work? Understand their current situation and stability.
  • Why are they looking to make a change? Identify the pain point or catalyst.
  • Are they looking to improve their lives? What does success and improvement mean to them?

Rapport-Building Best Practices

Setters: Focus on building initial trust, showing genuine interest in their situation, and making them feel heard. Your goal is to qualify them and move them to a Closer call.

Closers: Deepen rapport by actively listening to their responses, asking follow-up questions, and connecting their answers to our program's value. Guide them toward a confident decision.

Salesforce Data Logic

Lead Information

Lead Owner

Field that designates who is managing the lead (Setter or Closer). When a Lead schedules a call, the Setter manually updates this field to the assigned Closer's name.

Method of Contact

The channel we received the Lead's information (phone, text, web form, Facebook, etc.)

Original Source

Market attribution populated by Hubspot—represents the first trackable touchpoint

Lead Decision Field

Determines our interest in the lead based on entrance requirements:

Blank

Default for all leads except those with "Application_Approved" status. Remains blank until we evaluate fit or lead completes final stage of application.

Accept

We want the lead. Auto-set to "Accept" if Application_Approved, or manually set if lead verbally confirms they meet enrollment criteria.

Pending

We are pending approval for a lead based on pending reasons: graduation documents, wait list, board decision, or criminal record review.

Reject

We do not want the lead because they do not meet our requirements and cannot attend our course. Requires verbal/text confirmation from lead and must align with reject reasons.

Pursuit Field

Represents the current level of communication and engagement:

Active

A lead has either been created or re-engaged within the last 365 days.

Inactive

Either a lead has not talked to us after 365 days of being created/re-engaged OR they meet one of our inactive reasons: bad phone number, expressed disinterest, no location within 100 miles, only wants financial aid, other, rejected, scheduling conflict, unwilling to commute, seeking dental procedure, enrolled in another program, long code opt out.

Data Logic for Active Leads

Lead Owner Allowed Lead Statuses
Setter No Contact, Communicating, or Enrolled
Closer (name) Chase Call or Enrolled

Data Logic for Inactive Leads

If Lead Status = Inactive: Lead Owner can ONLY be Orphan
If Decision = Rejected: Lead Status must also be Reject, must have a reject reason, Pursuit must be Inactive, and Lead Owner must be Orphan

Lead Status (Position in Process)

Status Definition
New No contact yet; no outreach beyond automated initial text
No Contact Lead hasn't responded to any outreach attempts
Communicating Scheduled call with Closer; meaningful 2-way conversation but no verbal commitment to enroll
Chase Call Spoke with Closer; expressed clear intent/verbally committed to enrolling but hasn't completed enrollment
Enrolled Paid deposit & signed enrollment agreement
Rejected Doesn't meet enrollment criteria

Application Status (Enrollment Process Stages)

Status Definition
Applying Only progressed through initial portion; insufficient info to qualify
Application_Approved Progressed through Step 1; adequate info to accept
Application_Rejected Answered "No" or "In Progress" to education requirement
Application_Prevented Answered "Yes" to criminal conviction question
Onboarding Paid enrollment fee & signed agreement; hasn't signed Class 1 documents
Active Paid fee, signed all documents; fully enrolled
Enrollment_In_Progress Clicked "Complete Enrollment"; processing not yet finished

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Scheduling a Call (Key Procedure)

Steps:
  1. Go to Event Calendar in Salesforce (click Calendar tab)
  2. Find Closer's availability
  3. Schedule call, title it: "Call @ TIME" (@ sign MUST be included for reporting)
  4. Set Type: Call
  5. Set Duration: 15 minutes
  6. Attach the Lead to the event
  7. Relate it to the correct Closer
  8. Change Lead Owner to Closer's name
  9. Change Lead Status to Chase Call
  10. Turn Ava off

If Lead Doesn't Answer Call

Log as: "Call @ TIME - LVM" (left voicemail) or "Call @ TIME - NVM" (no voicemail)
Then:
  1. Change Lead Status back to Communicating
  2. Change Lead Owner back to Setters
  3. Turn Ava on
  4. Create Setter task: "Reschedule Call"

Opt Out SOP

Scenario: Lead texts "STOP"

If Ava is ON:
  • Ava should automatically make the lead Inactive
  • Set Inactive Reason: "Long Code Opt Out"
  • Turn herself off

If Ava is OFF:
  • Human handling must execute this process:
  • Change Lead Status to Inactive
  • Set Inactive Reason: "Long Code Opt Out"
  • Confirm Ava is off

Re-engagement SOP for Chase Call Leads

When Closer stops pursuing a Chase Call lead:

If the Closer has executed all follow-ups and no longer wants to pursue a Chase Call lead (typically because they stop responding but haven't explicitly expressed disinterest):

  1. Keep Pursuit = Active
  2. Change Lead Status to Communicating
  3. Change Lead Owner to Setters
  4. Turn Ava on
